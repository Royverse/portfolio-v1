Privacy policy

Camera and hand tracking

When you turn on hand tracking, your browser asks for camera permission. Video frames are processed locally by Google MediaPipe Hands, which runs as WebAssembly inside your browser tab. Only the resulting hand-landmark coordinates are used, frame by frame, and they are discarded immediately. No frame, image, landmark or recording is ever uploaded. You can revoke camera permission at any time in your browser's site settings. The Studio never requests your microphone.

The Studio uses your browser's localStorage. Nothing here is sent to us, and you can delete it by clearing site data for roymootsana.art.

License activation

When you activate a license key, your browser sends the key, a short device label (your browser's platform name, e.g. "Win32" or "MacIntel") and any previous activation ID to our verification function. The same check repeats about every two weeks while you're online. That function forwards the key to the payment provider that issued it (Polar or Gumroad) to confirm it is valid. It then returns a digitally signed receipt. The function has no database and does not keep your key. Our host may briefly keep standard request logs (see below).

Security

As an extra safeguard, an in-app guard refuses outgoing fetch, XHR and beacon requests to domains outside a short allow-list. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the amount of personal information we handle to a minimum.

Safety notes

  • Protect your hearing. Start with low system volume, especially on headphones. Use the Audio Kill switch (top bar) to silence everything instantly.
  • Give yourself room. Playing in mid-air involves sweeping arm movements. Clear the space around you and take breaks to avoid fatigue or strain.
  • Supervise children using the camera and audio.
  • The Studio is a creative tool. It is not a medical, therapeutic or accessibility device.

Instruments & trademarks

Every sound in the Studio is generated live by original synthesis code. No recordings, sample libraries or manufacturer ROMs of the original instruments are used. The instruments are interpretations inspired by these pioneers:

  • Theremin: invented by Leon Theremin around 1920.
  • Ondes Martenot: invented by Maurice Martenot in 1928.
  • Cristal Baschet: created by Bernard and François Baschet in 1952.
  • Handpan: a family of instruments that grew out of the Hang®, created by Felix Rohner and Sabina Schärer of PANArt in Bern, Switzerland, in 2000. Hang® is a registered trademark of PANArt Hangbau AG.
  • Laser harp: pioneered by Bernard Szajner and popularised by Jean-Michel Jarre.
